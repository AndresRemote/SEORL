🔒 Privacy & compliance

Are Medical Virtual Assistants HIPAA Compliant?

They can be, when training and access are set up the right way. HIPAA governs how protected health information (PHI) is accessed and shared, so compliance depends on both the assistant and the systems they work in.

Here's how every medical virtual assistant we place works with your practice. Want to walk through your setup? Talk to our team.

🎓
HIPAA training
Trained on HIPAA basics, including how to handle PHI, before they start.
✍️
Signed NDA
Every assistant signs a confidentiality agreement before day one.
🖥️
Your systems, your rules
They work inside your HIPAA-compliant tools (EHR, phones, secure messaging), so patient data stays in your environment.
🔑
Limited access you control
Role-based permissions for only what the job needs, and you can revoke access at any time.
